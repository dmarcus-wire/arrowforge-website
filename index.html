What's inside

✓

Goal countdown + coaching

Set your hunt opener or competition date. ArrowForge counts down and tells you what to focus on — plenty of time, final stretch, or game week.

✓

Training → Destination correction

Practice at sea level, hunt at 9,000 ft. ArrowForge accounts for the air density shift so your numbers reflect where you actually shoot.

✓

Build Match Score

A weighted readiness score tuned to your application — lethality matters more for hunters, trajectory and tuning for competitors.

✓

Transparent math

Every number shows the formula behind it. Tap any result to see exactly how it was calculated — no black boxes.

✓

Multiple builds

Save and compare setups side by side. Switch between your hunting rig, target setup, and practice arrows in seconds.

✓

Progressive ballistics

Starts useful with just draw weight and arrow weight. Gets more accurate as you add diameter, FOC, and advanced data.
