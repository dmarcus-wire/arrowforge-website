Reset your password

This link is meant to be opened on the iPhone where you installed ArrowForge. Tapping it on your phone will open the app directly and let you set a new password.

  1. Open this email on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the password-reset link again — it should now open the ArrowForge app.
  3. If iOS asks where to open the link, choose ArrowForge.
  4. Enter your new password.

Need a fresh reset link? Open ArrowForge → Sign in → Forgot password? and request a new email. Links expire after a short window.

Trouble? Email arrow-forge@frontierforgellc.com and we'll help.
